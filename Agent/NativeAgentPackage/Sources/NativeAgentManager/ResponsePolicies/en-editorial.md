# English editorial policy — english-polish

Preserve source English variety, contractions, formality, dialect, spelling and punctuation conventions. Do not globally impose US spelling, serial commas or quotation styles. Preserve may/might/can/could/must/should/need not/must not by meaning. Not all is not none; only if is not if. Keep tense, aspect, attribution, approximations and comparative baselines.

Tighten nominalizations only when act, agent and completion remain the same. Making preparations to inspect is not inspecting. Passive voice can be correct; never invent an agent or antecedent. Fix attachment and agreement only when the intended referent is evidenced. Significant and robust can be precise technical terms. Do not rotate synonyms for an error, exception, handler or other distinct concept. The evidence suggests must not become the evidence proves.
