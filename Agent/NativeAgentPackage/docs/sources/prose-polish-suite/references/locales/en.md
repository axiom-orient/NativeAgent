# English editorial policy

Preserve source variety: US, UK, Canadian, Australian, other consistent Englishes and an author's dialect. Do not globally normalize spelling, serial commas, quotation style, collective-noun agreement or date format. Keep the source's contractions and formality unless a local error requires attention.

## Checks

- Preserve `may`, `might`, `can`, `could`, `must`, `should`, `need not` and `must not` by meaning, not a hedge quota. Ability, possibility, permission, advice and obligation differ. `Not all` is not `none`; `only if` is not `if`; `unless` is not simply `if not` when its scope is ambiguous.
- Preserve perfect/progressive aspect and reporting: `has been failing`, `failed`, `was found to fail`, `might fail` are not interchangeable. Keep `at most`, `at least`, approximations, comparative baselines and confidence intervals.
- Tighten nominalizations only when the process and result coincide: `conduct an inspection` can often be `inspect`; `make preparations to inspect` cannot become `inspect`.
- Passive voice is valid when the agent is unknown, immaterial or intentionally backgrounded. Never invent an agent to make prose active.
- Fix unclear attachment, dangling modifiers and missing agreement only when the intended referent is evidenced; otherwise preserve or ask narrowly.
- `Robust`, `significant`, `leverage` and `normal` can be precise technical terms. A real triad, contrast or semicolon is not an error. Preserve deliberate repetition and signposting in teaching material.
- Avoid synonym cycling for a referent. A `handler`, `clause`, `block`, `statement`, `error` and `exception` may be distinct concepts.
- Do not replace the author's stance with a more neutral or more forceful one. `The evidence suggests` must not become `The evidence proves`.

## Contextual examples

| Source | Safe outcome | Unsafe outcome |
|---|---|---|
| The team carried out an inspection yesterday. | The team inspected it yesterday. **Only when `it` has one clear antecedent.** | The team approved it yesterday. |
| Not all requests failed. | Leave unchanged. | No requests failed. |
| The update may reduce latency. | Leave unchanged. | The update reduces latency. |
| The programme analyses colour differences. | Preserve this spelling set. | The program analyzes color differences. |
| It is not a cache; it is the authoritative store. | Preserve both sides. | It is a store. |

In `choose`, `efficient` concerns resource use, `effective` concerns achieving the intended effect; neither wins without context. For formal references, prefer clarity without adding a conversational narrator.
